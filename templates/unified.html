THE CORE IDEA

Understand the ground.
Then consider the flight.

A connected workflow from regional observation to aircraft-specific research recommendations.

01 / OFFLINE KNOWLEDGE

Build once. Keep observing.

Combine regional boundaries, elevation, satellite observations and mapped context. Derive candidate polygons, slope, surface and clearance evidence. Validate each dated observation before making it current.

02 / ONLINE ASSESSMENT

Match evidence to a mission.

Apply aircraft dimensions and mandatory terrain checks, then compare supported weather, distance and mission factors. Inspect the ranked polygons, reasons and missing evidence in one place.

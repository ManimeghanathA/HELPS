- “HELPSs proposes a helicopter-independent terrain knowledge framework”

The inspected search results did not reveal a clear matching passage for these samples. Some searches returned loosely related material rather than exact matches. This finding is limited to the sampled phrases and the results inspected; it does not establish that the entire report is free from unattributed overlap.

A local sentence-level repetition check found no identical repeated sentences of 15 words or more in the revised body text, before the contents field was refreshed. That check measures repetition within the document, not plagiarism against outside sources.

Selected literature details were cross-checked against public publication records, including [Meng and Chen's autorotation study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1000936113001775), [Mountrakis and Heydari's Landsat study](https://researchconnect.suny.edu/en/publications/harvesting-the-landsat-archive-for-land-cover-land-use-classifica/), and [Chen and Yue's DEM error study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S009830041000083X). The geometric distinction between a centroid and the pole of inaccessibility is also documented in [Mapbox's Polylabel reference](https://github.com/mapbox/polylabel). This was not a complete reference-by-reference bibliographic audit.

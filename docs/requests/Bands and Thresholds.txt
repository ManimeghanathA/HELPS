Bands and Thresholds 

10m resolution:
Band 2 (Blue): 490 nm — Used for atmospheric correction and land cover mapping.

Band 3 (Green): 560 nm — Used for vegetation discrimination and water body mapping (essential for indices like MNDWI).

Band 4 (Red): 665 nm — Used for vegetation mapping and identifying chlorophyll absorption (essential for NDVI).

Band 8 (NIR - Near Infrared): 842 nm — Highly sensitive to vegetation biomass and moisture content (essential for NDVI).

20m resolution
Band 5 (Vegetation Red Edge 1): 705 nm — Tracks early vegetation stress and chlorophyll changes.

Band 6 (Vegetation Red Edge 2): 740 nm — Used for calculating advanced vegetation status.

Band 7 (Vegetation Red Edge 3): 783 nm — Provides deep insights into canopy structure and health.Band 8a (Narrow NIR): 865 nm — Used for specialized vegetation indices and atmospheric correction.

Band 11 (SWIR 1): 1610 nm — Used for measuring soil and vegetation moisture, and detecting surface water (essential for MNDWI).

Band 12 (SWIR 2): 2190 nm — Used for geology mapping, burn severity (like dNBR), and mineral identification.

60m resolution

Band 1 (Coastal Aerosol): 443 nm — Used for monitoring coastal waters, dust, and smoke.

Band 9 (Water Vapour): 945 nm — Used for atmospheric water vapour correction.

Band 10 (SWIR - Cirrus): 1375 nm — Exclusively used for cloud detection (specifically high, thin cirrus clouds).


1. NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) — Used to measure vegetation greenness and health:

NDVI = B8 (NIR) - B4 (Red) / B8 (NIR) + B4 (Red)

value - -1 to 1
bare soil / rock - 0 to 0.1
sparse vegetation - 0.1 to 0.3
Dense Vegetation - 0.4 to 1

2. NDWI - Normalized difference water index
NDWI = B3 (Green) - B8 (NIR) / B3 (Green) + B8 (NIR)

Thresholds: 
Generally, values > 0 represent water bodies, while negative values represent soil and terrestrial vegetation.

3. MNDWI (Modified Normalized Difference Water Index) 
It improves upon the traditional NDWI because urban infrastructure (like concrete, asphalt, and rooftops) often reflects light in ways that mimic water bodies in the Green and Near-Infrared (NIR) spectrums. By replacing the NIR band with the Short-Wave Infrared (SWIR) band, MNDWI successfully separates water from urban background noise

MNDWI = Green - SWIR/ Green + SWIR

water bodies - 0.0 - 1.0
urban land and vegetation- < 0.0
